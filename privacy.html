Privacy Policy

Last updated:Friday, 25 September 2026. This applies to the MukMatch website only.

Plain-language notice. This policy was drafted with AI assistance and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before opening MukMatch to real students, have a lawyer check it, and confirm with Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office whether MukMatch must register as a data controller under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

Who runs MukMatch

MukMatch is an independent, student-run project. It is not operated, endorsed, or reviewed by Makerere University. The person or people who run it are the "we" in this policy. Contact: waltermichael594@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is stored securely by our database provider and is never visible to us in plain text.
  • Profile information: your name, gender, who you're looking for, age, college, year, hall or residence, interests, and your "ideal date" prompt.
  • Photos: up to four photos you upload. They are kept private, not public on the open internet, and only shown to other signed-in students on the same terms as the rest of your profile.
  • Activity: who you like, pass on, and match with; your messages with matches; your RSVPs to events; your reactions and posts on Campus Buzz, including posts marked anonymous.
  • Reports and safety records: if you report someone, or someone reports you, that report is kept, along with a record if a profile is ever removed for breaking the rules.

We don't collect payment details, and we don't buy data about you from anyone else.

How we use it

  • To run the core features: showing you profiles, creating matches, and enabling chat.
  • To keep the community safer: reviewing reports and removing accounts that break the rules.
  • To run events and show who's interested in them (organisers can see who RSVPs; other students only see a headcount).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who can see what

  • Other students can see your profile and photos, unless you've blocked each other. They cannot see your email, password, or reports involving you.
  • Anonymous Campus Buzz posts hide your name from other students, and also from the people who run MukMatch. Nobody can look up who posted anonymously.
  • The people who run MukMatch ("organisers") can see reports, the profile of the person reported, and event RSVPs. Organisers cannot read private messages between students through the app. This is a deliberate limit, not a promise about every possible situation: our database provider could technically be asked to access data, for example to comply with the law.
  • Our service providers host the data behind the app: our database and file storage provider, and our email provider (used only to send confirmation codes and password reset links). We do not control where these companies locate their servers, and your data may be stored outside Uganda.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you delete your account in the app, your profile, photos, matches and messages are permanently removed. Reports you were involved in are summarised in a private safety log so patterns of harm can still be reviewed, but your profile itself is deleted.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and photos at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from the You screen in the app.
  • You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing waltermichael594@gmail.com.

Age

MukMatch is meant for people aged 18 and over. We rely on you to tell us your age truthfully; we don't independently verify it. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including database rules that restrict who can read or change data, and private, non-public photo storage. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee data will never be exposed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MukMatch changes. If we make a significant change, we'll aim to let students know inside the app.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: waltermichael594@gmail.com.
